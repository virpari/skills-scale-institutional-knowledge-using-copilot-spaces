## Project Management Framework Overview

OctoAcme uses a structured, five-phase lifecycle to deliver value iteratively with clear ownership and a focus on continuous improvement. Projects begin with **Initiation**, validating business needs, defining goals, and aligning stakeholders. In **Planning**, the team breaks down work, estimates scope, and creates a prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done. **Execution** emphasizes daily standups, structured workflow via project boards, and automated as well as manual quality checks. **Release** phases use defined checklists for deployment, ensuring that all requirements are met, that CI/CD pipelines are passing, and rollback plans are in place. After each major event, a **Retrospective** is conducted to capture learnings and drive iterative improvements.

Core roles ensure effective delivery and shared accountability: **Project Managers** coordinate schedules, risks, and communications; **Product Managers** define and prioritize the work; **Developers** implement, test, and help shape solutions; **QA/Testing** ensures features meet quality standards; and **Stakeholders** provide input and approvals. Transparent communication—through weekly and monthly updates, risk registers, and escalation paths—keeps all team members and stakeholders aligned. The framework embeds quality assurance, customer focus, and psychological safety throughout, fostering reliable outcomes and knowledge sharing for current and future teams.
